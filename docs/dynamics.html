01 / MECHANISM

Forces, then motion.

Pairwise softened inverse-square gravity drives each body. A symmetric velocity Verlet step integrates the system at a fixed interval of 0.002 simulation units.

02 / VERIFICATION

Reproducible, not magic.

Source tests check momentum, pairwise symmetry, determinism, energy drift and close approaches. Live numerical drift is shown above and does not represent astronomical error bars.

03 / PERFORMANCE

Two rendering budgets.

Quality targets up to 60 FPS; Efficiency targets up to 30 FPS. The simulation stepping is identical. Observed FPS is a local browser measurement, not a GPU benchmark.
